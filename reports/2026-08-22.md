## INSIGHTS
* **Multi-Agent Teams Drive Scalable Solopreneurship** — Running specialized, 
named agents with individual cloud environments and robust agent-to-agent 
delegation allows a single builder to coordinate complex operational tasks 
without manual oversight [1-7].
* **Model-Agnostic Harnesses Slash Production Costs** — Deploying open-source 
developer harnesses with free, anonymous models on Open Router enables solo 
builders to run high-volume background tasks at zero cost, reserving premium 
models for highly polished deliverables [8-16].
* **MCP Connectors Unlock Autonomous Production Pipelines** — Integrating 
developer tools with creative suites like Higsfield via Model Context Protocol 
lets builders automate the generation of on-brand marketing videos and 
interactive web apps directly from workspace directories [17-21].

## TREND
The defining trend is the rapid shift from conversational AI chat assistants to 
autonomous, file-aware developer harnesses and multi-agent platforms that 
execute end-to-end business operations, coding, and content generation directly 
inside local workspaces [1-3, 8, 22-25].
